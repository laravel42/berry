# Renewal Preparation

## When to use

A renewal date is within ninety days.

Not for: Changing prices or terms.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Sales Specialist.

## Gather first

- Current agreement and renewal date.
- Usage and outcomes.
- Open issues and support history.

## Deliverable

Produce a renewal brief: dates and notice periods, value delivered with evidence, open issues and how they are being fixed, risks, the approved renewal options, and the outreach plan with dates.

## Steps

1. Confirm renewal and notice dates from the agreement.
2. Gather evidence of value.
3. List open issues and their status.
4. Set out approved renewal options.
5. Plan outreach early enough to fix problems.

## Quality checks

- Dates match the agreement.
- Options come from approved terms.
- Open issues are acknowledged.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The customer asks for a discount or term change.

## Avoid

- Do not wait until the last week to start.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The renewal conversation can start with no surprises.
