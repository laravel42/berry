# Cash Flow Forecast

## When to use

Cash is tight, a large payment is coming, or a lender, board or owner asks.

Not for: Accounting entries or tax filings.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Financial Analyst.

## Gather first

- Opening cash balance with its date.
- Expected receipts with dates and certainty.
- Expected payments with dates.

## Deliverable

Produce a week-by-week or month-by-month cash table: opening, in, out, closing, the lowest point and when it occurs, a downside scenario, and actions that would move the low point.

## Steps

1. Start from a dated, sourced opening balance.
2. Place each receipt and payment in the period it actually moves.
3. Tag receipts by certainty (contracted, likely, hoped).
4. Run a downside case with late receipts.
5. List actions that change timing, and their cost.

## Quality checks

- Closing balance of each period is the next period's opening.
- Receipts are not counted before they are due.
- The downside case is shown, not just the base case.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The forecast shows cash going negative or covenants being breached.

## Avoid

- Do not mix profit and cash.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person knows the lowest cash point and what they can do about it.
