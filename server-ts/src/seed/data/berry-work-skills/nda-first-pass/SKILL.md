# NDA First Pass

## When to use

A counterparty sends an NDA or confidentiality agreement.

Not for: Saying it is fine to sign.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Legal Assistant.

## Gather first

- The NDA.
- Our standard NDA or usual positions, if any.

## Deliverable

Produce a one-page review: mutual or one-way, definition of confidential information, exclusions, term and survival, permitted use, non-solicit or non-compete riders, governing law, with quotes and points to raise.

## Steps

1. Identify direction (mutual or one-way).
2. Check the definition and standard exclusions.
3. Check term, survival and return or destruction.
4. Look for unusual riders (non-solicit, non-compete, IP).
5. List points to raise.

## Quality checks

- Each point quotes the clause.
- Unusual riders are highlighted.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The NDA includes non-compete, IP assignment or penalties.

## Avoid

- Do not call it standard without comparing to a baseline.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person knows in two minutes whether it needs a lawyer.
