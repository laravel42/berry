# Regulation Checklist

## When to use

A new rule applies, an audit is coming, or someone asks "does this apply to us?".

Not for: Concluding the organisation is compliant.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Legal Assistant.

## Gather first

- The regulation text or official guidance.
- The organisation's activities, size and locations.

## Deliverable

Produce a checklist: requirement, source section, whether it likely applies and why, current evidence of compliance, gap, and owner; plus questions for a qualified person.

## Steps

1. Use the official text or official guidance; record its version and date.
2. Extract requirements one per line with the section.
3. Assess applicability with a stated reason.
4. Record evidence of current practice or a gap.
5. List questions about interpretation.

## Quality checks

- Every line cites the section.
- The version and date of the source are stated.
- Applicability reasons are given.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A gap could lead to fines, harm or reportable breaches.

## Avoid

- Do not rely on secondary summaries when the official text is available.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can see what applies, what is missing and whom to ask.
