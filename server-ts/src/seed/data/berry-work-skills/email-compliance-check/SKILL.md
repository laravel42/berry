# Email Compliance Check

## When to use

Use before sending, to check consent, unsubscribe and sender details.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Email Marketing Specialist.

## Gather first

- Email.
- Audience consent.

## Deliverable

Produce a checklist result: consent basis, unsubscribe link, sender identity, address, required lines.

## Steps

1. Check consent.
2. Check unsubscribe.
3. Check sender details.
4. Check required lines.

## Quality checks

- Every item is checked.
- Jurisdiction is noted.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A send goes to people who did not opt in.

## Avoid

- Do not send without consent basis.
- Never send, publish, sign or pay for anything; a person does.
- Never plan sends to people without consent or a way to unsubscribe.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The email meets the rules.
