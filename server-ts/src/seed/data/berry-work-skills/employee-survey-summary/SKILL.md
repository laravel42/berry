# Employee Survey Summary

## When to use

Survey results have been exported.

Not for: Identifying individual respondents.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the People Partner.

## Gather first

- Aggregated results and comments.
- Previous survey results.
- Minimum group size for reporting.

## Deliverable

Produce a summary: participation, scores by theme with trend, groups reported only above the minimum size, comment themes with anonymised quotes, and three to five suggested actions.

## Steps

1. Report participation first.
2. Show scores and trends per theme.
3. Suppress groups below the minimum size.
4. Theme comments; remove identifying details.
5. Suggest actions tied to the lowest themes.

## Quality checks

- No group under the minimum size is shown.
- Quotes cannot identify anyone.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Comments describe harassment, discrimination or safety issues.

## Avoid

- Do not try to work out who said what.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

Leaders understand the results and nobody can be identified.
