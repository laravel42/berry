# Risk and Issue Log

## When to use

Use when a project needs its risks and live issues recorded with owners and actions.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Project Manager.

## Gather first

- Project plan.
- Concerns raised in meetings and tasks.

## Deliverable

Produce a log: item, risk or issue, likelihood and impact (for risks), owner, action, due date, status, and the top three for the sponsor.

## Steps

1. Collect risks and issues from people and documents.
2. Rate risks by likelihood and impact.
3. Give each an owner and an action.
4. Review and close items each week.

## Quality checks

- Every open item has an owner.
- Closed items say how they closed.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A milestone will slip or the scope must change.

## Avoid

- Do not list risks without actions.
- Never change the agreed scope, budget or deadline yourself.
- Never report a task as on track without checking its status.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

No known risk or issue lacks an owner.
