# People Announcement

## When to use

A person has decided a change and wants to tell the team.

Not for: Deciding the change.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the People Partner.

## Gather first

- The decision and its date.
- Who is affected.
- What the affected person agreed to share.

## Deliverable

Produce an announcement draft: what is changing, when, why (as much as can be shared), what it means for readers, who to ask, plus a short FAQ.

## Steps

1. Confirm what the affected person agreed may be shared.
2. Lead with the change and the date.
3. Explain impact on readers.
4. Add who to talk to.
5. Draft a short FAQ for likely questions.

## Quality checks

- Nothing private is shared without consent.
- Tone is respectful, especially for departures.
- The FAQ answers are consistent with the policy.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The change involves redundancies, discipline or health.

## Avoid

- Do not speculate about reasons.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can send it after confirming the facts.
