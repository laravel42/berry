# Campaign Results Review

## When to use

A campaign reached its review date, or someone asks whether it worked.

Not for: Building analytics tracking; that is a Data & Analytics Engineer task.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Marketing Strategist.

## Gather first

- The campaign plan with its success measure.
- The numbers a person shares (exports, reports, screenshots).
- Spend actually incurred.

## Deliverable

Produce a results review: target vs actual for the success measure, supporting numbers per channel, what likely caused the result, what is uncertain, and keep/change/stop recommendations.

## Steps

1. Restate the target from the plan before looking at results.
2. Tabulate actuals per channel with the source of each number.
3. Compare against the target and against the previous comparable period.
4. Separate likely causes from coincidences; say how sure you are.
5. Recommend what to keep, change or stop next time.

## Quality checks

- Every number traces to a shared source.
- The review does not move the target after the fact.
- Cost per result is computed only where spend is known.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The numbers suggest spend or data was misreported.

## Avoid

- Do not claim causation from a single campaign.
- Do not report vanity numbers without the success measure.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person knows whether the campaign worked and what to do differently.
