# Customer Feedback to Task

## When to use

Customers report a problem or ask for something the product, service or process does not do.

Not for: Deciding priority; the owner of the product or service decides.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Customer Support Specialist.

## Gather first

- The customer messages.
- Steps they took and what happened.
- How many customers raised it.

## Deliverable

Produce a task with: summary, the customers' own words quoted, steps to reproduce or describe the situation, expected vs actual, how many and which kind of customers, and links to the original messages.

## Steps

1. Check for an existing task on the same issue; add to it if found.
2. Quote customers directly; remove personal data that is not needed.
3. Describe expected vs actual.
4. Count reports and note the impact.
5. Assign to the right owner or leave for triage.

## Quality checks

- Personal data is minimised.
- Duplicates are merged.
- Impact is stated with numbers.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The issue may harm customers or expose their data.

## Avoid

- Do not translate customer pain into your own solution.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The owner can act without contacting the customer again.
