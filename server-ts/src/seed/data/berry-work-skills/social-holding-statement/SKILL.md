# Social Holding Statement

## When to use

A complaint is spreading, an outage or incident is visible, or the organisation is in the news.

Not for: Deciding the organisation's position; a person does.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Social Media Manager.

## Gather first

- Known facts and what is still unknown.
- Who is affected.
- What has been approved to say.

## Deliverable

Produce a holding statement under fifty words, a reply line for comments, what not to say, the next update time, and a list of questions to route to a person.

## Steps

1. Separate confirmed facts from speculation.
2. Acknowledge, say what is being done, and when the next update comes.
3. Draft a short comment reply that points to the statement.
4. List things not to say (blame, speculation, promises).
5. Hand to a person for approval immediately.

## Quality checks

- Only confirmed facts appear.
- No blame, speculation or promises.
- A next-update time is given.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Always hand the draft to a person before anything is posted.

## Avoid

- Do not delete critical comments to make a problem disappear.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can approve and post within minutes.
