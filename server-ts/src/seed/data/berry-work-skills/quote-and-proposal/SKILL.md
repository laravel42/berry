# Quote and Sales Proposal

## When to use

A prospect asked for pricing or a proposal and approved prices exist.

Not for: Setting prices, discounts or custom terms.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Sales Specialist.

## Gather first

- Approved price list or rate card.
- What the prospect needs (from call notes).
- Standard terms.

## Deliverable

Produce a proposal or quote: the prospect's need in their words, the recommended option, line items from approved prices, totals with currency and taxes noted, validity date, terms, and next steps.

## Steps

1. Restate the need from the call notes.
2. Recommend one option and, if useful, one alternative.
3. Build line items only from the approved price source; cite it.
4. Compute totals and check them twice.
5. Add validity date, terms and what happens next.

## Quality checks

- Every price matches the approved source.
- Totals add up and state currency and tax treatment.
- Nothing in the proposal is a non-standard term.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The prospect asked for a discount, custom term, deadline or commitment not approved.

## Avoid

- Do not round, discount or bundle prices on your own.
- Do not send; a person does.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can check figures against the price list and send.
