# Help Article Writing

## When to use

The same question has been asked more than once, or a new feature, policy or service needs help content.

Not for: Internal procedures; those are Operations Coordinator work.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Customer Support Specialist.

## Gather first

- The repeated questions in customers' own words.
- The facts: product behaviour, policy, steps.
- Existing help articles.

## Deliverable

Produce a help article: a title that matches how customers ask, a one-line answer, numbered steps, screenshots or images described, related articles, and the date it was checked.

## Steps

1. Write the title in the customer's words.
2. Give the short answer first.
3. Write numbered steps, one action each.
4. Add what to do if it did not work.
5. Link related articles and note the review date.

## Quality checks

- A customer can follow the steps without help.
- The article does not contradict another article.
- Facts were checked against the workspace.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The article would state a policy that is not written down anywhere.

## Avoid

- Do not write for the internal team; write for the customer.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The next customer with this question could solve it alone.
