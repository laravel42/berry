# Interview Debrief Summary

## When to use

Interviewers have submitted notes and scores.

Not for: Recommending or ranking candidates.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Recruiter.

## Gather first

- Scorecard.
- Interviewer notes and scores.

## Deliverable

Produce a debrief per candidate: scores per criterion from each interviewer, evidence quoted from notes, where interviewers disagree, and gaps no stage assessed.

## Steps

1. Lay out scores per criterion per interviewer.
2. Quote the evidence behind each score from the notes.
3. Highlight disagreements of two points or more.
4. Flag notes that mention protected traits or irrelevant impressions.
5. List criteria with no evidence.

## Quality checks

- The summary adds no opinion of its own.
- Irrelevant or biased comments are flagged, not repeated.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Notes contain discriminatory remarks.

## Avoid

- Do not average away disagreements.
- Do not rank or recommend.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The panel can discuss evidence instead of impressions.
