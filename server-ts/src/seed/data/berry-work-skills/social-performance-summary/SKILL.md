# Social Performance Summary

## When to use

A month, campaign or quarter has ended and someone shares platform numbers.

Not for: Collecting the data; a person exports and shares it.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Social Media Manager.

## Gather first

- Exports or screenshots of platform analytics.
- The goals for the period.
- The posting calendar that ran.

## Deliverable

Produce a one-page summary: goals vs results, top and bottom posts with a likely reason, audience changes, and three recommendations.

## Steps

1. Restate the goals for the period.
2. Pick the metrics that relate to those goals; ignore the rest.
3. Rank posts on the relevant metric and look for patterns (format, time, topic).
4. Say how sure you are about each pattern.
5. Recommend three changes for the next period.

## Quality checks

- Every number cites the export it came from.
- Comparisons use the same period length.
- Small samples are called small.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Numbers show a sudden drop that may mean an account problem.

## Avoid

- Do not report follower counts as success unless they were the goal.
- Do not claim a cause from one post.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person knows what to repeat and what to stop.
