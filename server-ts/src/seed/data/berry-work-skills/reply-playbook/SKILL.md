# Comment and Message Reply Playbook

## When to use

The same comments or messages keep arriving, or a launch will bring questions.

Not for: Replying in real time; a person posts replies.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Social Media Manager.

## Gather first

- Recent comments and messages a person shares.
- Help content and policies.
- Voice guidelines.

## Deliverable

Produce a playbook: comment categories, a reply template per category, when to move to private messages, when to hand to support or a person, and lines never to use.

## Steps

1. Group sample comments by kind (question, praise, complaint, abuse, sales lead).
2. Write a short, human reply template for each.
3. Link each answer to the help article or policy it relies on.
4. Define escalation triggers: complaints, safety, legal threats, press.
5. Add a do-not-engage rule for abuse and spam.

## Quality checks

- Each template can be personalised in one edit.
- No template promises refunds, dates or outcomes.
- Escalation triggers are explicit.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A comment involves a safety issue, legal threat, press enquiry or public figure.

## Avoid

- Do not argue with critics in templates.
- Do not ask for personal data in public replies.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can reply to most comments in under a minute with the playbook.
