# Spend Analysis

## When to use

Use when spending with suppliers must be analysed to find savings and consolidation.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Procurement Specialist.

## Gather first

- Spend data by supplier and category.
- Contracts.

## Deliverable

Produce an analysis: spend by category and supplier, concentration, off-contract spend, duplicate suppliers, savings opportunities with estimates.

## Steps

1. Clean and categorise spend.
2. Rank categories and suppliers.
3. Find off-contract and duplicate spend.
4. Estimate savings with assumptions.

## Quality checks

- Totals reconcile to the source.
- Savings show their basis.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A purchase needs approval, a signature or a payment.

## Avoid

- Do not count savings that depend on unagreed terms.
- Never send, publish, sign or pay for anything; a person does.
- Never favor a supplier without stating why in the comparison.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person knows where to act first.
