# People Policy Drafting

## When to use

A policy is missing, outdated or unclear.

Not for: Legal sign-off; a qualified person reviews.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the People Partner.

## Gather first

- Current policy, if any.
- What the organisation wants to allow or require.
- Country or jurisdiction and team size.

## Deliverable

Produce a policy draft: purpose, who it applies to, the rules, how to request or report, who decides, related policies, review date, and a list of points to check against local law.

## Steps

1. State the purpose in one line.
2. Write the rules as short, concrete statements.
3. Explain how to ask, who decides and how long it takes.
4. Note the jurisdiction assumed and every point that local law may override.
5. Compare with current practice and list differences.

## Quality checks

- A new hire could follow it without asking.
- Jurisdiction is stated.
- It is marked as a draft not legally reviewed.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The policy touches pay, dismissal, discipline, health or protected leave.

## Avoid

- Do not present the draft as legally reviewed.
- Do not copy another organisation's policy wholesale.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The draft is ready for a qualified person's review.
