# Supply Risk Map

## When to use

Use when the supply chain's single points of failure must be identified.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Supply Chain Coordinator.

## Gather first

- Suppliers per item.
- Lead times and locations.

## Deliverable

Produce a risk map: item, suppliers, dependency level, risks (single source, long lead, region), impact, and mitigations.

## Steps

1. List critical items and their sources.
2. Flag single sources and long lead times.
3. Rate impact.
4. Propose mitigations.

## Quality checks

- Critical items are all covered.
- Mitigations are practical.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A shortage will delay customer orders.

## Avoid

- Do not ignore low-cost items that stop production.
- Never place an order or change a delivery; a person does.
- Never present a figure, quote or source you did not take from the work in front of you.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

Critical supply risks are known and owned.
