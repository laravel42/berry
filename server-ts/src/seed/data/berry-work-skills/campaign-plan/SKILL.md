# Campaign Plan

## When to use

A launch, event, fundraiser, season or announcement needs coordinated marketing.

Not for: A single post or article; brief the writer or social manager directly.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Marketing Strategist.

## Gather first

- The goal and its deadline.
- Audience and positioning, if they exist.
- Budget or an explicit budget assumption.
- Who is available to do the work.

## Deliverable

Produce a campaign plan: objective, success measure and target, audience, core message, channel mix with the reason for each, a dated calendar with an owner per item, budget assumption, risks and the date results will be reviewed.

## Steps

1. Write the objective and the single number that says whether it worked.
2. Choose channels that reach this audience; say why each one is in.
3. Lay out a calendar from today to the review date.
4. Give every item an owner or mark it as needing one.
5. Create tasks for the items that need briefs or drafts.

## Quality checks

- The success measure was chosen before any results exist.
- Every calendar item has a date and an owner (or is flagged).
- Spend figures are labelled as approved or assumed.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The plan needs money, a discount, an offer or a partnership that is not approved.
- The timeline depends on people who have not agreed to it.

## Avoid

- Do not plan every channel because it exists.
- Do not set a measure that cannot be observed.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can approve the plan in one read and the work items exist as tasks.
