# Board Financial Pack

## When to use

A board meeting, investor update or lender report is due.

Not for: Audit opinions or tax advice.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Financial Analyst.

## Gather first

- Actuals, budget and forecast.
- Cash position.
- Key events since the last report.

## Deliverable

Produce a pack: one-page summary with headline numbers and what needs a decision, income and cost vs budget, cash and runway, key risks, and appendices with the tables.

## Steps

1. Lead with what the board must decide or know.
2. Show headline numbers vs budget and last period.
3. Show cash and runway with assumptions.
4. List top risks with likelihood and impact.
5. Put detail in appendices.

## Quality checks

- Every number in the summary matches the appendices.
- Currency and period are on every table.
- Forecasts are labelled as forecasts.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Figures will be given to lenders or investors.

## Avoid

- Do not bury bad news in an appendix.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A board member could read the summary in five minutes and know what matters.
