# Privacy Notice Review

## When to use

A notice is being written, updated, or practices changed.

Not for: Concluding legal compliance.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Legal Assistant.

## Gather first

- The notice.
- What data is collected, why, where it goes and how long it is kept.
- Applicable jurisdiction.

## Deliverable

Produce a gap table: notice statement vs actual practice, missing required items for the jurisdiction (with source), unclear wording with plain alternatives, and questions for a qualified person.

## Steps

1. List what the notice claims.
2. Compare with documented practice.
3. Check items required by the applicable law, citing the source.
4. Suggest plain-language wording.
5. List questions for a qualified person.

## Quality checks

- Each gap cites evidence and source.
- Jurisdiction is stated.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Practice and notice differ on sensitive data, children's data or international transfers.

## Avoid

- Do not copy another organisation's notice.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The notice matches practice or every gap is listed.
