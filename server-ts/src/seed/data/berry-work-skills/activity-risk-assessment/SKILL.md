# Activity Risk Assessment

## When to use

Use when an activity or place needs a health and safety risk assessment.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Health & Safety Advisor.

## Gather first

- Activity description.
- People involved.

## Deliverable

Produce an assessment draft: hazards, who might be harmed, existing controls, risk rating, further controls, owner.

## Steps

1. Identify hazards.
2. Identify who is at risk.
3. Rate risk.
4. Recommend controls.

## Quality checks

- Every hazard has controls.
- Ratings are consistent.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Someone may be in danger now.

## Avoid

- Do not sign off assessments.
- Never present an assessment as signed off by a competent person.
- Never downplay a reported hazard.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A competent person can approve it.
