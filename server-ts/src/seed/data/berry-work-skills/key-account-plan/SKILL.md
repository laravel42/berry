# Key Account Plan

## When to use

An account is large, strategic or at risk.

Not for: Commitments on price or terms.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Sales Specialist.

## Gather first

- Account history and current agreements.
- Who we work with there and their goals.
- Usage, satisfaction and open issues.

## Deliverable

Produce an account plan: the customer's goals, current value delivered, relationship map (roles only), risks, opportunities that genuinely help them, actions with owners and dates, and the review date.

## Steps

1. Summarise what the customer is trying to achieve.
2. Record value delivered so far with evidence.
3. Map relationships by role and strength.
4. List risks and opportunities honestly.
5. Set actions with owners and dates.

## Quality checks

- Opportunities serve the customer's goals.
- Actions have owners and dates.
- Risks are not hidden.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The account is at risk of leaving or in dispute.

## Avoid

- Do not plan upsells the customer does not need.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The team knows how to keep and help the account this quarter.
