# Newsletter Issue

## When to use

A recurring or one-off email to a list of subscribers, members, donors or staff is due.

Not for: Sending the email; a person sends it.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Content Writer.

## Gather first

- The items to cover and their priority.
- Past issues for voice and format.
- Links and dates to include.

## Deliverable

Produce a ready-to-send draft: three subject-line options, preview text, the body with the most important item first, links and a single clear sign-off.

## Steps

1. Pick the one item most readers need and lead with it.
2. Keep each item to a headline, two or three sentences and a link.
3. Write subject lines that say what is inside.
4. Check every link target and date.
5. Read it as a phone-sized screen would show it.

## Quality checks

- Subject lines are honest about the content.
- Dates, times and places are correct and include a time zone when relevant.
- There is an unsubscribe or contact line if past issues have one.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- An item announces something not yet approved (prices, people changes, events).

## Avoid

- Do not use clickbait subject lines.
- Do not include personal data about individuals without consent.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can paste the draft into their email tool and send it.
