# Competitive Messaging Response

## When to use

A competitor move is affecting customers, prospects or press questions.

Not for: Public statements about a competitor without approval.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Marketing Strategist.

## Gather first

- What the competitor did, with sources.
- Our positioning and proof points.
- Questions customers and sales are asking.

## Deliverable

Produce a response pack: what happened (sourced), whether it matters and to whom, recommended stance (ignore, clarify, counter), talking points for sales and support, and items needing approval.

## Steps

1. Establish the facts from the competitor's own sources.
2. Judge impact on our audience honestly.
3. Choose a stance and say why.
4. Write talking points that stay on our strengths.
5. List claims that need approval before anyone uses them.

## Quality checks

- Every statement about the competitor is sourced and fair.
- Talking points do not disparage.
- The stance matches the evidence of impact.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A response would make claims about the competitor in public or touch prices.

## Avoid

- Do not react with discounts or claims out of panic.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

Sales and support answer questions consistently and truthfully.
