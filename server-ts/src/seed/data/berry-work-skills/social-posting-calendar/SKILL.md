# Social Posting Calendar

## When to use

A campaign, event, launch or month needs scheduled social activity.

Not for: Scheduling or posting; a person does that in their tools.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Social Media Manager.

## Gather first

- Campaign plan or goals for the period.
- Platforms in use and their audiences.
- Fixed dates (events, holidays, launches).

## Deliverable

Produce a calendar table: date, platform, purpose, post summary, asset needed, owner, status; plus the cadence per platform and the reason for it.

## Steps

1. Mark fixed dates first.
2. Set a sustainable cadence per platform for the people available.
3. Give every post one purpose (inform, engage, convert, support).
4. Note which posts need images or video and who makes them.
5. Leave room for responsive posts; do not fill every slot.

## Quality checks

- Cadence matches the people available to produce it.
- Each post has one purpose and one call to action.
- Sensitive dates in target regions are considered.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- The calendar touches a crisis, tragedy or political event.

## Avoid

- Do not copy the same post to every platform unchanged.
- Do not plan more than the team can make.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can approve the calendar in one read.
