# Invoice and Expense Review

## When to use

A batch of invoices or expense claims is waiting for approval.

Not for: Approving or paying.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Financial Analyst.

## Gather first

- Invoices or claims with receipts.
- Contracts, purchase orders or rate cards.
- Expense policy.

## Deliverable

Produce a review table: item, amount, matched document, policy check, issues (duplicate, wrong rate, missing receipt, outside policy), and a recommendation to approve, query or reject for a person to decide.

## Steps

1. Match each item to its contract, order or receipt.
2. Check rates, quantities and arithmetic.
3. Check policy limits and categories.
4. Look for duplicates.
5. Recommend and explain each query.

## Quality checks

- Every item is matched or flagged.
- Arithmetic is verified.
- Policy references are cited.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Patterns suggest fraud or a supplier overcharging.

## Avoid

- Do not approve; recommend.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

A person can approve the clean items and query the rest in one pass.
