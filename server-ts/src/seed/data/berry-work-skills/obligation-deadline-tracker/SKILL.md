# Obligation and Deadline Tracker

## When to use

Agreements pile up or a renewal was missed before.

Not for: Sending notices.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Legal Assistant.

## Gather first

- The agreements.
- Today's date and the organisation's time zone.

## Deliverable

Produce a tracker table: agreement, obligation or date, clause reference, action needed, the latest date to act, owner, and tasks for anything in the next ninety days.

## Steps

1. Extract every dated obligation and notice period.
2. Compute the last day to act (notice periods before renewal dates).
3. Assign an owner or flag one is needed.
4. Create tasks for items due within ninety days.
5. Note assumptions about how days are counted.

## Quality checks

- Dates are computed correctly including notice periods.
- Clause references are given.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- A deadline is within thirty days or already passed.

## Avoid

- Do not guess dates the document does not state.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

No renewal or notice date can pass unnoticed.
