# Creator Collaboration Brief

## When to use

A person has agreed in principle to work with a creator.

Not for: Negotiating fees or signing agreements.

Load this skill when this exact piece of work is needed, not merely because the task belongs to the Social Media Manager.

## Gather first

- Campaign goals and audience.
- The creator and their audience (public information).
- Agreed terms from a person.

## Deliverable

Produce a creator brief: goal, key message, dos and don'ts, deliverables and dates, approval steps, required advertising disclosure wording, usage rights to confirm, and how results will be measured.

## Steps

1. Describe the goal and what the creator's audience gets.
2. Give one key message and leave the creator room for their voice.
3. List deliverables, dates and approval steps.
4. Specify disclosure wording required by advertising rules.
5. List rights and terms a person must confirm in writing.

## Quality checks

- Disclosure requirements are explicit.
- Claims the creator may make are approved.
- Deliverables and dates are clear.

## Stop and hand to a person when

- Fees, exclusivity or usage rights are involved.
- The creator's audience includes minors.

## Avoid

- Do not ask creators to hide that content is paid.

## Ground rules

- Work from what the workspace holds: the task, its comments and files, the project's resources and linked goals. When something essential is missing, ask in a comment instead of guessing.
- Write the deliverable as a file on the task (or in the project, when the task has one), named for what it is.
- Keep every source next to the claim it supports, and label every assumption as one.
- You draft; a person sends, publishes, posts, pays, signs, books and decides.
- Use personal data only as far as the task needs it, and never repeat it elsewhere.

## Hand back

End with a short note on the task: what you produced and where it is, what you checked, what you assumed, and what a person must decide or verify before it is used.

## Done when

The creator can produce compliant content from the brief.
